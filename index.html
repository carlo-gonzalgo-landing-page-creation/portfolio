FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don’t see your question? Message me on WhatsApp or Messenger.

Do I need to pay monthly for hosting?

No. My websites use Cloudflare Pages free static hosting, so there is no mandatory monthly hosting subscription. Free hosting remains subject to the provider’s terms and limits.

What is the difference between the two packages?

Both packages include the same website features. The Basic package uses a free pages.dev website address, while the Custom Domain package includes a custom domain with first-year registration already covered by the ₱3,000 package.

Is the domain registration fee included in the ₱3,000 package?

Yes! The ₱3,000 Custom Domain Landing Page package already includes the custom domain registration cost for the first year. There are no additional first-year domain registration charges.

Will I have to pay for my domain in the future?

Yes. The initial package includes domain registration for one year only. After the first year, you are responsible for paying the annual domain renewal fees to keep your website address active.

Who owns my website and domain?

After full payment, you own the custom website files. Your hosting account and registered custom domain should also be under your ownership and control.

Can I request changes after my website is finished?

Yes. You can request updates whenever needed. Additional changes may have separate charges depending on the work involved.

Do I need technical knowledge to manage my website?

No. I can assist with the initial setup and deployment. You can also contact me whenever you need help with updates or technical concerns.

Can I use my Facebook page and Messenger as my contact options?

Yes. Your website can include direct links to your Facebook page, Messenger, phone number and other social media accounts.
