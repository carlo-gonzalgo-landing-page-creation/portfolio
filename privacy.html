Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version: this website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. I only receive your personal information if you choose to message me, and I use it to reply to you and discuss your project. I don’t sell it.

1. Who I am

This website is run by Carlo Gonzalgo, a web designer based in the Philippines who makes HTML landing pages for car-care businesses. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean Carlo Gonzalgo, and I am the person responsible for the personal information described here.

2. What information I collect

Information you give me. The contact form on the home page asks for your name, business type, country and a short message. You may also share more when you chat with me, such as your business name, phone number, social media profile and project details.

How the form works. The form doesn’t send your details to a server of mine. When you press “Send via WhatsApp” or “Send via Messenger”, your browser builds a message from what you typed and opens WhatsApp or Messenger. Nothing is sent to me until you press send inside that app. For Messenger, the message text is also copied to your device’s clipboard so you can paste it.

Information collected automatically. I don’t run analytics, cookies, advertising pixels or tracking scripts on this site. However, the services that host and deliver the site (listed in section 4) may receive technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.

3. How I use your information

  • To reply to your message and answer your questions.
  • To prepare quotes and discuss, plan and deliver a landing page project you ask for.
  • To keep a record of our conversation so I can follow up on the work.
  • To protect the website and comply with the law, if needed.

I use your information because you gave it to me to ask about my services (your consent) and because it is needed to respond to your request.

5. Who I share it with

I don’t sell or rent your personal information. I only share it with the tools I need to talk with you and run my work (such as the messaging apps above), or when the law requires it.

6. How long I keep it

I keep messages and project details for as long as I need them to respond to you, deliver a project and handle any follow-up questions. You can ask me to delete your information at any time (see section 7).

7. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to correct it, to object to its use, to ask for it to be deleted or blocked, to receive a copy, and to claim damages if you were harmed by misuse. If you live elsewhere, such as in a US state with its own privacy law, you may have similar rights.

To use any of these rights, contact me using the details in section 11. If you’re unhappy with how I handled your information, you can also complain to the Philippines’ National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS, which encrypts the connection between your browser and the page. I keep conversations in my own accounts on the messaging apps above. No system is completely secure, so please don’t send passwords or payment card details in your messages.

9. Sample pages

The landing pages in my portfolio are concept projects for fictional businesses. Their business names, addresses, phone numbers and prices are made up, and their forms are demos that don’t send or store anything. Please don’t enter real personal information into them. To reach me, use the contact form on the home page.

10. Children

This website is meant for business owners and isn’t directed at children. I don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Contact me

For privacy questions or requests, message me on WhatsApp (+63 995 233 1416), Messenger or LinkedIn.

12. Changes to this policy

If I change how this site handles information, I’ll update this page and change the “Last updated” date at the top.
